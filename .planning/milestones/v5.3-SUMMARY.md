# v5.3 — Slide Scripts & Q&A Preparation

## Scope

Give the presenter talking-point speaking cues for all main defense slides and a comprehensive Q&A preparation document covering every aspect of the report, so they could defend the work confidently and in their own words — directly addressing a judge's informal "reads as AI-generated" feedback. Emergency milestone; defense held 2026-07-15. The thesis report itself stayed locked and was not modified by this milestone.

This milestone ran unusually long past its formal phase close (Phase 34, 2026-07-14) because the actual defense didn't happen until the next day, and iteration continued right up to — and during — the defense itself. That tail is real project history and is captured below, not just the original Phase 34 scope.

## What shipped

**Phase 34 (2026-07-14):**

- `defense_speaking_script.md` — talking-point cues for all main slides, timed to `33-RUN-PLAN.md`'s budget
- `defense_qa_preparation.md` — topic-organized Q&A (data pipeline, QLoRA hyperparameters, quantization rationale, evaluation, the task_scam recall-recovery story, design rationale, and an explicit "AI-generated" authorship-challenge section) — later found to number the checkpoint-584 derivation, t-test literacy section, and quick-reference table among its most-used sections during live prep

**Same-day and next-day slide iteration (untracked as formal phases, executed directly given the deadline):**

- Demo section (Sample Output + Demo video placeholder) cut from the timed main flow to the backup appendix to protect the 10-minute budget, then Sample Output reinstated to the main flow (end of Evaluation Results, before Conclusion) per a later call that it was worth the ~35s as real supporting evidence — Demo itself stayed cut, backup-only

## Defense outcome (2026-07-15)

Defense completed. Judges' feedback, captured for the next milestone:

- Report quality was acknowledged as good but criticized as short — "bare minimum pages."
- A specific, pointed gap was raised live: the exact threat-class labels used during training could not be found in the report ("where is the Label you chosen during training, cant even find it").
- A judge directly requested a report revision: "if you did the report, i hope you revise it."
- Judges repeatedly asked "did you include this in the report" across multiple topics — report/slide/artifact traceability is clearly a real scoring axis for this panel, not incidental.

## Decision: slides are now LOCKED

No further slide edits are planned. The next milestone is a report revision, scoped once the student supplies the actual judge transcripts/notes from the defense — not started yet, deliberately, to avoid guessing at scope ahead of the real feedback.
