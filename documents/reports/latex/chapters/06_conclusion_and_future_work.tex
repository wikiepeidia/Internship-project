This thesis implemented and evaluated a local, text-only system for Vietnamese financial-phishing detection. The completed work joins a governed four-label corpus, two locally trained model families, a verified Qwen Q8\_0 portable inference artifact, one terminal shared-cohort evaluation, and a local command-line and browser-demo runtime. The evidence supports a working academic prototype. It does not support a claim of production readiness or performance on the distribution of real Vietnamese scam traffic.

\section{Main Findings}

The final governed corpus contains 2{,}097 messages: 1{,}658 training, 219 validation, and 220 terminal-evaluation rows. The release checks record valid schemas and suspicious-text spans, group-level seed disjointness across the three partitions, duplicate control, all-label support, and the declared seed-concentration cap. The content remains synthetic-derived and model-assisted. In particular, 296 surviving reconstructed Zalo messages share the same model family as the final quality judge, so that subset is not generator-family-independent.

The corpus-quality evidence combines complete automated coverage with bounded human corroboration. The joined judge bundle covers 2{,}097 of 2{,}097 rows and records 1{,}395 passes (66.52\%). A separate stratified 100-row human review records 44 PASS and 56 FAIL, with judge and human decisions agreeing on 87 of 100 rows. The 44/100 result is evidence about that deliberately stratified sample; it is neither a random population estimate nor a claim that 56\% of the complete corpus is defective. Agreement also measures consistency between two decision sources, not ground-truth accuracy.

Two fresh full models were trained locally on the same 1{,}658-row training and 219-row validation identities with one predeclared seed (42). The Qwen path used genuine 4-bit NF4 QLoRA; the PhoBERT path used a fully trained, non-quantized four-class classification head. On the shared validation cohort, the selected Qwen checkpoint reached macro F1 0.9885 and the selected PhoBERT checkpoint reached 0.9849, both with zero invalid outputs. These are single-run development-validation observations. They do not estimate run-to-run variance or statistical significance, and the generative Qwen path and discriminative PhoBERT path are not comparable as throughput benchmarks.

The selected Qwen adapter was merged and converted to a 4{,}280{,}403{,}232-byte Q8\_0 GGUF artifact. Its portable receipt binds the complete SHA-256 identifier reproduced in Appendix~1 and records both the original and an independent load smoke. This verifies the Qwen GGUF materialization path. It does not imply that PhoBERT was converted to GGUF, nor that either model has completed deployment fitting.

One terminal shared-cohort evaluation then processed all 220 evaluation rows with both frozen models. Qwen QLoRA reached accuracy 0.981818, macro F1 0.980493, and weighted F1 0.981848. PhoBERT reached accuracy 0.990909, macro F1 0.990892, and weighted F1 0.990925. Both produced zero invalid outputs and one risky-to-benign error. PhoBERT was higher on the recorded aggregate metrics, bank-impersonation recall/F1, and Zalo precision/F1; Qwen was higher on benign precision/recall/F1. This is an honest measured advantage on one cohort, not evidence of stable superiority. The result was terminal: it did not trigger retraining, repair, threshold changes, checkpoint selection, or another evaluation pass.

The implemented system also now has a clearer maintained architecture. The model-adaptation CLI facade was reduced from 1{,}329 to 232 lines while preserving its characterized command contract. An 886-line source-archiving utility became a 160-line compatibility facade over bounded domain modules. The checked policy describes 29 active modules and 83 exact active edges, and the public application is the three-command \texttt{vnphish} interface. This refactor improves navigation and separation of responsibilities; it did not generate or replace the frozen training and evaluation results.

\section{Limitations}

The strongest limitation is external validity. The corpus is Vietnamese, text-only, and substantially synthetic or model-assisted. It excludes screenshots, QR codes, audio, and multi-turn interaction. Its labels and messages therefore cannot reproduce the full distribution of real victim communications, changing criminal tactics, regional language, or platform-specific context. A real-world, independently collected and human-labelled evaluation remains necessary before operational use.

The human evidence is intentionally bounded. The 324-row triage targeted judge-flagged candidates, while the later 100-row review was stratified to include labels, judge outcomes, and provenance origins. Neither process is a simple random audit of every row. The 87/100 judge--human agreement is useful corroboration, but it is not inter-annotator reliability across multiple independent human experts and is not a substitute for population-level quality estimation.

The model experiment used one training seed. It therefore provides no empirical variance, confidence interval, or significance result. No t-test or stable-winner claim is justified. In addition, Qwen produces structured generative output while PhoBERT produces class logits; their quality scores can be compared on the shared label task, but their latency and throughput paths are structurally different and were not admitted as a fair speed comparison. The older 254-row Qwen study is retained only as historical background and is not authority for the final corpus or terminal result.

The terminal cohort was excluded from training, tuning, thresholding, and checkpoint selection, but it was not globally untouched at the filesystem level. Corpus-quality work had exposed its content before model evaluation, and the provenance correction records at least two broad automated pre-run integrity reads plus one focused post-run read. Those reads performed no model inference, external-service call, human row display, retraining, selection, or repair. The accurate claim is one terminal two-model evaluation pass, not zero prior filesystem access.

Deployment fitting was explicitly deferred. The Qwen GGUF receipt and local runtime demonstrate materialization and local execution, not a production deployment, calibrated operating threshold, monitored service, or new unbiased score after fitting. PhoBERT remains a local classification artifact rather than a GGUF deliverable.

Finally, the architecture refactor is substantial but not security-closed. The current review records six critical and three warning findings involving the guarded filesystem/native boundary, pre-collection attestation, command-route discovery, loopback-demo request validation, terminal control-sequence handling, shell-chain scanning, bind validation, and test-launch configuration. Until those findings are resolved and re-reviewed, the codebase should not be described as fully secure, fully closed, or production-ready.

\section{Ethical Considerations}

The system is designed to classify and explain suspicious text, not to generate persuasive scam messages. Its output contract limits the user-facing result to risk, threat labels, grounded cues, and protective recommendations. Even so, a detection model can be wrong, and a confident interface may create a false sense of safety. The prototype should therefore support human verification rather than replace it.

The corpus was constructed from public warning material and synthetic generation rather than private victim conversations. This reduces direct privacy exposure, but it also narrows realism and can reproduce generator or judge biases. The recall-first emphasis reflects the higher practical cost assigned to missed financial scams, while false positives still matter because they can create alarm and unnecessary verification. These trade-offs should be reported with class-level errors rather than hidden behind one aggregate score.

\section{Future Work}

The immediate engineering priority is to close the nine recorded architecture-review findings, repeat the focused security review, and retain an explicit residual-risk statement. This work should harden the local demo and terminal rendering without changing or re-attributing the frozen experimental results.

The main scientific priority is an independently collected, human-labelled real-world dataset with documented consent, privacy handling, time period, platform context, and difficult cross-class overlaps. A new external cohort can test generalization without replaying or tuning against the terminal 220-row result. Multiple training seeds and a predeclared analysis can then quantify variance and determine whether observed model differences persist.

A later deployment fit may train on all permitted labelled development data, but it must be presented as a separate deployment artifact. It cannot inherit the terminal score as an unbiased evaluation of that fitted model. Calibration, operational thresholds, monitoring, rollback, and human-escalation policy would also need independent validation.

Optional extensions include OCR for image-forwarded alerts and broader language coverage. Each extension changes the error surface: OCR introduces Vietnamese-diacritic and layout errors, while multilingual expansion introduces new slang, cultural context, and scam mechanisms. Both should therefore receive their own labelled evaluations rather than being treated as cost-free preprocessing or transfer.

The appendices and the accompanying evidence map retain the result identities, limitations, failure-and-recovery chronology, and source paths used for this conclusion. They are intended to make the distinction between measured evidence, historical background, and deferred work auditable.
