\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This thesis presents a local-first pipeline for detecting Vietnamese financial phishing messages and returning a structured label, risk tier, grounded evidence, and safety guidance. Its first contribution is a governed 2{,}097-row, four-class corpus with seed-group split controls, exact-span validation, post-generation quality evidence, targeted human triage, and explicit lineage quarantine. The 324-row triage produced 91 drops and 233 approved relabels; only 57 relabels were admitted because 176 otherwise acceptable rows shared one non-independent lineage. A separate review found systematic narrative framing in 240 retained Zalo rows. These rows were replaced through controlled offline, model-assisted reconstruction from 60 preserved semantic roots, with five variants per root and zero external API calls; four reconstructed rows were later quarantined, leaving 296 reconstructed rows in the promoted corpus. The final quality bundle records 1{,}395/2{,}097 judge passes (66.52\%), while a separate 100-row Vietnamese review records 44 passes and 87/100 human--judge agreement.

The second contribution is a measured laptop training and deployment study. A bounded ordinary-LoRA probe was technically runnable but reached 7{,}902/8{,}151~MiB device-memory use and projected 18.42--18.88 compute hours from an incomplete step window; no out-of-memory failure was observed. This resource evidence motivated QLoRA rather than a claim that LoRA is impossible. Fresh local training then completed for Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 with genuine NF4 QLoRA and for a non-quantized PhoBERT classification head. The selected Qwen model was exported as a verified Q8\_0 GGUF artifact; PhoBERT was retained in its native classifier format. One terminal shared-cohort evaluation over 220 records yielded macro F1 scores of 0.980493 for Qwen QLoRA and 0.990892 for PhoBERT, with zero invalid outputs for both. PhoBERT's measured advantage is reported descriptively, without a significance or stable-superiority claim. Automated integrity tests had accessed the split files before the terminal model run, but those reads displayed no row content, performed no model inference, and did not influence training, selection, repair, or retry. Finally, the maintained code was reorganized around named runtime, data, integrity, modeling, and evidence boundaries while preserving the historical metric-producing source. A dedicated threat audit verified all 48 declared mitigations, while a separate adversarial code review still records six critical and three warning findings; the broader architecture review is therefore not presented as closed. The prototype is not recommended for safety-critical deployment without evaluation on a real-world, human-labelled corpus.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Vietnamese phishing detection; local large language models; explainable artificial intelligence; QLoRA adaptation; GGUF inference; financial fraud prevention.
