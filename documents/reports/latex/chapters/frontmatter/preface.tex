\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This thesis presents a local-first explainable AI system for Vietnamese financial phishing detection. The implemented pipeline combines reproducible dataset construction, an offline text runtime, parameter-efficient local model adaptation, and a decision layer that returns risk tiers, threat labels, grounded cues, and safe user recommendations. The final internal evaluation covers 254 seed-disjoint held-out messages, with macro F1 of 0.9553. All three risky classes met their per-class recall targets, with task-scam recall reaching 0.871 on 62 held-out examples. However, given the synthetic-derived holdout, small sample size, and two outstanding explanation quality cautions, deployment in safety-sensitive settings is not recommended without real-world validation. The thesis contributes a working local analysis pipeline together with a transparent record of what was built, evaluated, and found to be limited.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Vietnamese phishing detection; local large language models; explainable artificial intelligence; QLoRA adaptation; GGUF inference; financial fraud prevention.
